6. Put It Into the Planner

A calendar full of due dates is not yet a study plan.

Step 1
Enter fixed class meetings and major exam dates.
Step 2
Enter recurring reading checks and problem sets, then override exceptions individually.
Step 3
For each midterm, add preparation blocks before exam week.
Step 4
Add before-class preview blocks where the syllabus requires reading before lecture.
Step 5
Add short after-class review/retrieval blocks around difficult units.
Step 6
Create a weekly ELMS check so schedule changes make it into the planner.
