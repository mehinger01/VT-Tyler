Live Lecture Capture

The during-class job isn't transcribing. It's capturing structure and marking gaps in real time, so there's something specific to work with after class instead of a vague sense of confusion.

The During-Class Job

Main ideas and structure, in your own words. Not full sentences, not every word the professor says.

The moment something moves too fast to fully catch. A quick flag now saves a much longer struggle later.

Capture the Lecture

Five fragments from a simulated lecture on the four functions of management. Write a short capture note for each — a phrase or two, not a transcript.

"Planning means setting goals and figuring out how to reach them. Think of a bakery deciding to expand into catering."

"Organizing means arranging people and resources to actually carry the plan out."

"Leading is motivating and directing the people doing the work — not just giving orders."

"Controlling means tracking whether things are going according to plan — she mentions KPIs quickly and moves on."

"These four functions overlap constantly in real companies" — followed by a fast real-world example that's hard to fully catch.

Mark What's Missing

For each fragment, be honest about whether it actually landed live.
