Session 6 · Make Learning Stick

Retention & Spaced Practice Lab

Move beyond familiarity. Build a study process that requires the brain to retrieve, repair, and revisit learning over time.

The Four-Step Retention Cycle

1. Learn

Understand the idea well enough to begin working with it.

2. Retrieve

Close the notes and bring the idea back from memory.

3. Check & Repair

Compare, identify gaps, and correct only what is missing or inaccurate.

4. Space & Repeat

Return after time has passed and retrieve it again.

Choose Your Retrieval Methods

Select methods that make you produce the answer rather than recognize it.

Apply It by Subject

Math

Recall the formula or process, identify when it applies, and solve without copying an example.

Science

Explain vocabulary, sequences, cause-and-effect relationships, diagrams, and applications in your own words.

Reading

Close the text and reconstruct the main idea, evidence, unfamiliar terms, and remaining questions.

Class notes

Use headings or questions as prompts, then retrieve the explanation before checking the notes.
